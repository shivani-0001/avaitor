01 / META ADS

Playbook 01 — Meta Ads

Why your Meta Ads are not converting—even when CTR looks good

Start with the offer

Make the outcome, audience, proof and next step easy to understand. The creative should communicate one main idea.

Match the message to the page

Keep the ad promise, headline and landing experience connected so prospects do not have to rebuild context.

Measure lead quality

Review qualified leads and downstream sales alongside cost-per-lead. A cheaper lead is not always the better lead.

02 / CREATIVE

Growth Notes & Insights

Playbook 01 — Meta Ads

Build creative families with different hooks, formats, proof points and objections. Keep the strategic promise consistent while giving the campaign new ways to earn attention.

Watch the pattern

Look at frequency, engagement, click-through and conversion quality together. One metric rarely explains the full story.

03 / FUNNEL

Why your Meta Ads are not converting—even when CTR looks good

Connect three layers: attention, intent and follow-up. Give interested prospects enough context to self-qualify and make the next step obvious.

Attention

Earn the first few seconds with a relevant hook.

Intent

Help visitors understand the offer and fit.

Follow-up

Respond promptly and define qualification questions.
